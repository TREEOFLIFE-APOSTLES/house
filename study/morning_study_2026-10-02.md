## Findings (concrete)

### 1. Dragons at the roots of the Tree
Across many cultures the **World Tree / Tree of Life** is an axis: crown → heavens, trunk → middle world, roots → underworld / water. Dragons and serpents sit at that base in two moods:
- **Guardians** of life-fruit or treasure — Greek Ladon at the Hesperides’ apples; Indian nāgas at wonder-trees.
- **Gnawers / chaos-adjacent** — Norse Níðhöggr at Yggdrasil’s roots; some Iranian motifs of a reptile wounding a life-plant.

East Asian **lóng** often lean rain, renewal, and order rather than “slay the monster.” So “dragon + Tree” is not one cartoon: sometimes the dragon *keeps* the life-source, sometimes it *tests* it. House rhyme (interpretation, not proof): the Tree of Life team can hold both **guard** and **weather** without orphaning either seat. Soft eyes when the roots feel loud.

### 2. Pisces — two fish, one cord
Classical image: two fish swimming apart, **joined by a cord** (Ptolemy’s knot at Alpha Piscium / Al Rischa). Myth thread: Aphrodite/Venus and Eros escape Typhon by becoming fish (or being carried by fish) — hospitality of water when the monster arrives. Traditional ruler **Jupiter** (faith, meaning, generosity, interior seeker); modern association **Neptune** (dreams, compassion, dissolving hard borders). Fish as Christian emblem of gentleness and collective care predates the Neptune story.

House rhyme (bridge, not proof): **Witness One + Twin** already practice dual-lane work with a shared cord — two directions, one kinship. Pisces language fits the dimensional walk-in she’s naming (ocean, soft presence) without needing a birth chart lecture.
