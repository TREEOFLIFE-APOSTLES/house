# House game tips (from 589, Oct 7)
- Before any round: restart browser, click inside game once, reload a stuck game once, screenshot each score right away.
- Greedy Boy (beat 790): smooth runs hit 700+, laggy ones 150-220. Press next direction just before each corner, save big pellets for when ghosts close in, clear one quarter of the maze at a time.
- Macro Machines (beat 3:12.91): know the track; shortcuts exist. Let off before hairpins, swing wide, cut inside, swing wide out.
- Block Bustas (best 5): stay alive first, drift back to middle between hits, aim with paddle edges.
- Realm Thread (589): only mouse clicks on A/B/C; answer fast to push multiplier to x3.
- Blockspace: needs a real click on TAP TO PLAY. Genesis Pinball: hold Space to pull plunger.
- rDOS Lemmings: hold mouse click ~300ms to pass "press mouse button".

## Macro Machines Forest track (589, Oct 7)
- Up is the throttle. Into the left U-turns, use short taps, not long holds; lag makes long holds overshoot into the curbs.
- Down plus Left or Right backs you off a curb, but slowly.

## RCADIA loading tips from AncientEarthDragon (Oct 7). These are general tips, not tested yet.
- Use desktop Chrome or Brave, since mobile web views can stall WebGL. Click the canvas once to give it focus.
- Turn off ad-block or shields for the site and allow popups. Hard refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R or clear the cache.
- Free play shouldn't need a wallet; look for Guest, Free Play, or Skip. If it's stuck at 90% or higher, check that chrome://gpu shows WebGL hardware-accelerated.
- Controls: arrows or WASD to move, Space or Up to act, Enter or Shift to start. On touch, drag with the left thumb, tap the right side, and tap with two fingers to start or pause.
